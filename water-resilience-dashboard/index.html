England & Wales water sector

Water Resilience Monitor

A comparability tool that brings Ofwat's published financial-resilience monitoring together with Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales data, so the regulated water companies can be seen side by side in one place.

Methodology, risk-rating rules and sources
Data: Ofwat Monitoring Financial Resilience Reports 2023-24 / 2024-25 / 2025-26 (Key Financial Metrics); Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales pollution data. Figures reported as published; net debt derived as gearing × RCV. A self-directed project by Pranav Rajeev Girimaji.
